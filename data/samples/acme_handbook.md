# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

_This is a fictional document used as demo content for the RAG chatbot._

## Working hours

The standard work week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00
in your local time zone; outside those hours you are free to arrange your
schedule as needed. Time sheets are not required for salaried employees.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. A fully remote
arrangement requires written approval from a Vice President and is reviewed
every 12 months. If you work remotely you must be reachable on Slack during core
hours.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year. PTO accrues
monthly and unused days do not expire at year end, but no more than 5 days may
be carried into the next calendar year. Sick leave is separate and is not
capped.

Parental leave is 16 weeks of fully paid leave for all new parents, which can be
taken any time within the first year.

## Equipment and home office

Every employee receives a company laptop, refreshed every 3 years. In addition,
there is a home-office stipend of $1,500 that resets every 2 years and can be
spent on a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days through Expensify. Your manager can approve
expenses under $500; anything $500 or above needs director approval. Travel
booked through the corporate portal does not need pre-approval if it is under
$2,000.

## Onboarding

New hires are paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 90 days. Your
buddy is not your manager and is there to answer day-to-day questions and help
you learn how the company works.
